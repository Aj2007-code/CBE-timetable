Choose your attendance style
This decides how sessions you don't tap on are counted. You can change it later from the Attendance tab, but switching will re-apply to your whole semester's record — so pick the one that matches how you actually want to track attendance.
✓Conventional
Mark every class yourself — Present, Absent, or Cancelled. Anything you forget to mark counts as absent.
⚡Auto-present
Every class is assumed present by default — you only need to tap a class if you were Absent or it was Cancelled.
You can switch modes anytime from the Attendance tab.

Know more about this page
A quick tour of what's here, beyond just your timetable.
SPI SPI Calculator — open the SPI tab, pick a grade for each of your courses this semester, then tap Calculate SPI once you're done. Your SPI out of 10 shows up instantly, along with a subject-by-subject breakdown.
MS Mid-Sem — the Mid-Sem tab shows your five CB21xx exam dates and your HSS elective's exam, with a countdown to the next one, plus the full week's datesheet if you need to check a clash.
CR Credits — the Credits tab lists the L-T-P (Lecture/Tutorial/Practical) split and credit value for every course this semester, plus your total credit load at the top.
✓ Attendance — mark Present, Absent, or Cancelled from the Now or Week view with one tap, then track your overall and subject-wise percentage in the Attendance tab. Choose your attendance style (Conventional or Auto-present) at login, or change it anytime from the Attendance tab — that's also where you can turn automatic cloud backups on or off.
ED Edit your day — in the Attendance tab, pick any date to add an extra/makeup class or remove one that's been cancelled — just for your own timetable, the shared schedule never changes. Removed classes can be restored anytime.
✦ Ask — tap the ✦ button in the corner to open a quick AI assistant. Ask it about your schedule, attendance, or electives and it'll give you a short, direct answer.
PYQ PYQ — open the PYQ tab, pick a course, and view or download its previous year question papers.
🔔 Announcements — tap the bell 🔔 at the top for important messages. Each one auto-disappears 6 hours after posting, and you can 👍 or 👎 any of them.
Notes — tap the Notes button at the top to open the shared notes drive folder. Source: CBE Offc Gr.
